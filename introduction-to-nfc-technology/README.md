## Introduction to Near Field Communication (NFC) Technology

### What is NFC?

Have you ever used Apple Pay or a tap to pay card? If so, you are already an NFC pro! Think of NFC as a tiny invisible bubble around you phone or payment card that can share information when it touches another bubble. Cool, right?

### How Does It Work?

NFC creates a super small wireless field (about 4cm or 1.6 inches) and uses electromagnetic fields to transmit data. Because of this field it interacts automatically when two devices are close enough. It doesn't sound like it, but this whole interaction requires very little power.

### The Tech Behind the Tap

- Operates at 13.56 MHz
- Transfer speeds up to 424 kbps
- Three modes:

  1. Card Emulation - paying with your phone
  2. Reader/Writer - scanning tags
  3. Peer-to-Peer - sharing between devices
